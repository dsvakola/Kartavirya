## 1. Introduction
Kartavirya – Reminder Companion ("we", "our", or "the application") respects your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when using the desktop application.

## 2. Information Collection and Storage
- **Offline & Local First:** Kartavirya operates entirely on your local personal computer.
- **No Personal Data Collected:** The application does not collect, track, profile, transmit, or sell any personal data, identifiers, or usage statistics.
- **Local Reminders and Notes:** All reminders, notes, misplaced item logs, and timestamps are stored strictly on your local disk in the application's local data storage directory. No remote server or third-party cloud service receives this information.

## 3. Network Access
Kartavirya does not require or utilize internet connectivity for its core functionality. When you click external informational links (such as to the Vidyasagar Academy website or email support), your default system web browser or mail client will handle the request externally according to your operating system settings.

## 4. Third-Party Services
Kartavirya does not integrate with any third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks.

## 5. Security of Your Data
Since all reminder data remains on your local storage device, security is maintained by your operating system's standard user permissions and access controls.
